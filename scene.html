THE MAKING OF THE VALLEY

Scale, atmosphere,
and a working railway.

Controls

Choose a camera view above. In Free orbit, drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and use two fingers to pan on a touchscreen.

Use Tab to reach controls and Enter or Space to activate buttons. Use arrow keys on the pace slider and train selector. Outside controls, Space plays or pauses the railway and H hides or restores the interface. Escape closes these notes.

If your device requests reduced motion, the railway starts paused. Press Play when you are ready.

Four services follow two mountain loops past Ravenfell Junction and Pinewatch. The original Python-built locomotives are reused here, with passenger coaches, a rocky tunnel, and a timber settlement.

The landscape, track, buildings, ferns, and rocks are generated from a fixed seed. EZ-Tree supplies branching trees and foliage; Poly Haven supplies CC0 mossy ground and cliff textures. Warm sunlight, soft shadows, moving steam, and layered haze tie the scene together.

Visual direction draws on the Nordic forests of God of War and Assassin’s Creed Valhalla. Their artwork and game assets are not included.

This is a browser scene with fixed-route train animation, not a railway physics simulator. Use High detail for sharper shadows and rendering, or Balanced for lighter GPU use.
